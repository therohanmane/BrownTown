Privacy notice

Draft describing how this website currently works. Pending review and approval by INBrownTown before launch. Last updated: 24 October 2026.

What this website collects

The website collects personal information only when you send a partnership enquiry through the form on the home page. The form asks for your name, business or organisation name, mobile number, and a message, and optionally your email, city and approximate budget. It also records the language you were using and the time you started filling in the form (used only to filter automated spam).

How your enquiry is used

  • Your enquiry is sent directly to the INBrownTown team so they can reply to you about advertising, creative or media-partnership work.
  • It is not sold, rented or shared with other businesses.
  • It is not saved in your browser. The website does not keep a copy of enquiries in a public database.
  • The team may keep your enquiry in its email or business records for as long as needed to respond and to manage any work you agree on.

News tips are separate

The enquiry form is for business enquiries only. Sending one does not result in news coverage. Editorial suggestions should be sent to INBrownTown on Instagram.

Embedded videos

Videos are played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play on a video. Once you do, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that player. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com).

Cookies and storage

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It stores two small preferences in your browser so they are remembered next time: your chosen language and your light/dark theme. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask INBrownTown to correct or delete an enquiry you sent. Contact details for privacy requests will be published here once confirmed by INBrownTown.
